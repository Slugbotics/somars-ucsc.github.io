Perception.

We teach the drone to see. A YOLO11n detector running under TensorRT on a Jetson Orin Nano Super, finding targets from the air with nobody at the controls.

About

Giving the drone
its eyes.

We are the Perception subteam. We write the computer vision that lets the drone see the ground below it and pick out the SUAS 2026 targets: a mannequin, a tent and a car. Our detector is Ultralytics YOLO11n, trained on Kaggle and deployed on the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super with TensorRT 10.3 in FP16. In simulation the same model runs through ONNX.

Day to day, that means working with the onboard camera, labeling flight imagery, rendering synthetic scenes, and keeping our ROS 2 nodes talking to the flight controller. It has to keep working at altitude during a fully autonomous flight, not just on a laptop at the bench.

Focus Areas

What we work on.

Target Detection & Classification

We train Ultralytics YOLO11n on Kaggle to find the three SUAS 2026 target classes (mannequin, tent, car) in aerial imagery, then run it on the Jetson Orin Nano Super with TensorRT 10.3 in FP16. We also keep an 8-class label set ready for future work.

Dataset Collection & Labeling

We label real flight frames locally in Label Studio. Two survey flights so far have given us 1,253 frames that we are working through. To fill the gaps, we render synthetic training scenes in Blender 4.2 and train on both together.

Camera & Calibration

Our camera is an Arducam Pivariety AR0234: color, global shutter, 1920x1200, read over V4L2. We recently fitted a 6 mm lens, calibrated it with a ChArUco board, and are retraining the detector for the new field of view.

Survey Mapping

During a survey, the drone streams photos to the ground station while it flies, and we stitch them into a map there. OpenDroneMap is an optional step when we want a fuller reconstruction.

Technology

Our stack.

Detection Model Ultralytics YOLO11n, trained on Kaggle on the three SUAS 2026 classes (mannequin, tent, car)
Inference TensorRT 10.3 in FP16 on the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super. ONNX for the same model in simulation
Camera Arducam Pivariety AR0234, color global shutter, 1920x1200 over V4L2, 6 mm lens calibrated with a ChArUco board
Flight Software ROS 2 Humble on the Jetson in our somars_vision package, talking to PX4 on the Pixhawk 6X over uXRCE-DDS with px4_msgs
Data & Labeling Label Studio for local labeling, real flight frames, and synthetic scenes rendered in Blender 4.2
Mapping Photos streamed to the ground during flight and stitched there, with OpenDroneMap as an optional step
