Mechanical.

We design, build and assemble the drone and its payload systems.

About

The physical drone,
start to finish.

If you can hold it in your hands, it's ours. That means the airframe that keeps the drone in the air, the payload bay that carries the mission equipment, and everything in between.

We draw parts in CAD, build them with the tools in SlugWorks, test them, and see what breaks. Then we fix it and try again. The goal is a drone that is light, strong, and ready for competition.

Focus Areas

What we work on.

Airframe Design

Arm geometry, motor mounts, center body, and landing gear. We want it light and stiff, and we want it to survive a hard landing.

Assembly & Integration

We put the drone together and work with Embedded Systems so the electronics, wiring, and sensors have a clean place to mount.

Payload Systems

The payload bay and drop mechanisms for competition missions, including the actuators that release a payload at a set of GPS coordinates.

Manufacturing

We make our own parts in the SlugWorks makerspace: 3D printing, carbon fiber layups, and CNC machining.
