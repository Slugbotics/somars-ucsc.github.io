Mechanical.

About

The physical drone,
start to finish.

If you can hold it, it's ours: the carbon fiber airframe, the payload winch, and the parts we make for both. We draw them in CAD, build them in SlugWorks, test them, see what breaks, and fix it.

Focus Areas

What we work on.

Airframe Design

Arms, motor mounts, center body, landing gear. Light, stiff, and built to survive a hard landing.

Assembly & Integration

We put the drone together and give Embedded Systems a clean place to mount electronics.

Payload Systems

Our two-bay winch lowers payloads on a tether. SUAS 2026: a water bottle to a mannequin, a beacon to a tent.

Manufacturing

Our own parts, made in SlugWorks: waterjet-cut carbon plates, 3D printing, CNC machining.
