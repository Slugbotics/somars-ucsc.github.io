Embedded Systems.

We build the electronics and flight software that let the drone fly a mission on its own.

About

Where hardware
meets software.

We own everything between airframe and mission: flight controller, companion computer, sensor buses, radios, and power, plus the ROS 2 code that ties them together. If any of it fails, the drone stays on the ground.

Focus Areas

What we work on.

Flight Controller Integration

We build PX4 with our own patches and tune it for stabilization, motor mixing, and radio control.

Sensors, Power & Communication

We wire the two DroneCAN buses for ESCs and sensors, plus the radios and the power system.

Companion Computer

The Jetson runs our ROS 2 autonomy, talks to PX4, and bridges MAVLink to the ground.

Autonomous Controls

We write the state machines and mission planners that fly waypoints, work the winch, and enforce the lost-link rules.
