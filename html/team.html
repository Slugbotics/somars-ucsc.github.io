Structure

Why subteams?

Building an autonomous drone takes mechanical engineering, electronics, real-time software, computer vision, and simulation. That is a lot for any one person. Subteams let each of us go deep on one part while we still work together on the whole platform.

Each subteam owns one core layer of the drone stack. We work in parallel, so progress keeps moving across the whole system.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I have to pick just one subteam?

Not necessarily. Most of us focus on one subteam first to build depth, then branch out once we're comfortable.

Can I switch subteams?

Yes. Talk with the subteam leads and we'll help you move over. People switch when their interests change or they want a new challenge.

How experienced do I need to be?

You don't need any drone or robotics experience. Being curious and showing up consistently matters far more than what you already know.

What majors are welcome?

All majors. We need mechanical engineers, computer scientists, electrical engineers, and anyone who is excited about autonomous systems.

Join Us

How to get involved.

01

Complete SlugWorks Canvas & join Discord

Most of our work happens in SlugWorks (BE-49). Complete the safety training Canvas module, then join our Discord so you hear about meetings and announcements.

02

Visit a few subteam meetings

Check meeting times on each subteam page. If the times don't work for you, reach out. We reschedule at the start of every quarter to fit everyone.

03

Commit to a subteam

Once you've looked around, pick a primary subteam and start taking on tasks: CAD, firmware, vision code, simulation scripts, whatever the group needs.
