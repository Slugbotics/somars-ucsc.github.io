The Team.

We want students from any major, experience or not, to build a drone with us.

Structure

Why subteams?

An autonomous drone needs mechanical, electronics, software, vision and simulation work, so each of us goes deep on one layer and we build the aircraft together.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I have to pick just one subteam?

Not for good. Most of us start on one, then branch out.

Can I switch subteams?

Yes. Talk with the subteam leads and we will help you move.

How experienced do I need to be?

None. Showing up and staying curious matters more than what you know.

What majors are welcome?

All of them. Anyone who wants to work on autonomous aircraft.

Join Us

How to get involved.

01

Complete Slugworks Canvas & join Discord

We work in Slugworks (BE-49). Complete the Canvas safety training module, then join our Discord for meetings and announcements.

02

Attend a general meeting

We meet Tuesdays 5:00 to 6:00 PM and Thursdays 6:00 to 7:00 PM in Slugworks (BE-49).

03

Commit to a subteam

Pick a primary subteam and take on tasks: CAD, firmware, vision code, simulation scripts, whatever the group needs.
