About Us

Student built. Fully autonomous.

SOMARS is the UC Santa Cruz drone team under Slugbotics. We design, build and fly aircraft that run a whole competition mission with nobody at the controls, and we do it as a team of students from every engineering major.

Competitions

One aircraft. Two missions.

Both competitions ask the same thing of us: fly a real mission with nobody at the controls.

01

C-UASC

Collegiate Unmanned Aircraft Systems Competition

Caligula I flew at Mojave, California in 2026. We return in June 2027 for six missions: waypoints, a timed circuit, package drop, delivery and recovery, and target localization.

Mojave, CA Next: June 2027

02

SUAS

Student Unmanned Aerial Systems Competition · AUVSI

Caligula II flew the full mission at Tulsa in September 2026: one command, then waypoint laps, a survey, target detection and a winch delivery inside 45 minutes.
